# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to a respectful, constructive, and safety-conscious community.

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Engage in good faith.
- Welcome newcomers and learners.
- Avoid harassment, abuse, or discriminatory conduct.
- Avoid contributions that encourage harmful use of drone or robotic systems.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior undermines community safety or project purpose.
