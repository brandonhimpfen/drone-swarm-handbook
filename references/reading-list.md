# Reading List

This starter list is intentionally category-based so maintainers can add verified references over time.

## Multi-agent systems

- Introductory material on distributed artificial intelligence and agent-based modeling.
- Research on task allocation, consensus, and coordination.

## Swarm intelligence

- Foundational texts on emergent behavior, collective intelligence, and decentralized coordination.

## Robotics and simulation

- Documentation for robotics middleware, simulation environments, telemetry logging, and scenario design.

## Safety and assurance

- System safety engineering references.
- Human oversight and accountable autonomy literature.
- Risk assessment frameworks.

## Governance

- Aviation authority guidance for unmanned aircraft systems.
- Privacy and data protection guidance for aerial sensing.
