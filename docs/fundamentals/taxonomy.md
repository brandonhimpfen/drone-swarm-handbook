# Drone Swarm Taxonomy

This taxonomy organizes drone swarm systems by design dimension.

## By control structure

- Centralized
- Decentralized
- Hybrid
- Hierarchical

## By autonomy level

- Manual multi-drone operation
- Assisted coordination
- Supervised autonomy
- Conditional autonomy
- High autonomy in constrained environments

## By communication model

- Direct agent-to-agent communication
- Ground-station-mediated communication
- Mesh communication
- Broadcast updates
- Event-triggered messaging

## By mission type

- Mapping and survey
- Environmental monitoring
- Infrastructure inspection
- Search-and-rescue support
- Agriculture
- Education and research
- Artistic or demonstration flights

## By environment

- Indoor controlled environments
- Outdoor controlled test areas
- Simulated environments
- Restricted research facilities
- Operational environments requiring formal permissions

## By system maturity

- Conceptual model
- Simulation prototype
- Hardware-in-the-loop prototype
- Controlled field test
- Certified or approved operational system
