# FAQ

## Is a swarm just many drones?

No. A swarm implies coordination. Multiple drones flying independently are a fleet, but not necessarily a swarm.

## Does a swarm require artificial intelligence?

Not always. Some swarm behavior can be rule-based, scripted, or algorithmic. AI may be used for perception, planning, prediction, or adaptation, but it is not mandatory.

## Should this handbook be used for real-world deployment?

No. This handbook is educational and simulation-first. Real-world flight requires qualified expertise, permissions, risk assessment, and compliance with applicable aviation rules.

## What should beginners study first?

Start with robotics fundamentals, coordinate systems, control theory basics, simulation, communication models, safety engineering, and multi-agent systems.

## What is the safest first project?

A simulation-only project that models a small number of agents, simple formation behavior, telemetry logging, and clear failure conditions.
