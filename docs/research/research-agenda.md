# Research Agenda

This agenda lists open research and documentation questions suitable for simulation-first exploration.

## Coordination

- How do different task allocation strategies compare under simulated agent failure?
- What coordination models are easiest to audit?
- How does communication delay affect formation stability?

## Safety

- Which safety constraints are most important in beginner simulations?
- How can safety events be represented in telemetry schemas?
- What review artifacts best support accountability?

## Human oversight

- How should human review gates be represented in mission definitions?
- What information should a supervisor see during simulation replay?
- How can uncertainty be communicated clearly?

## Documentation

- What page structure best supports newcomers?
- How should dual-use risks be documented without enabling misuse?
- What examples are useful while remaining safe and simulation-only?
