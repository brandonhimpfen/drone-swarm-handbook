# Literature Map

This page outlines research areas connected to drone swarm systems.

## Multi-agent systems

Studies how multiple autonomous or semi-autonomous agents interact, coordinate, and pursue goals.

## Swarm intelligence

Explores distributed behavior inspired by biological systems such as birds, insects, and fish.

## Robotics

Covers perception, control, planning, sensing, localization, and actuation.

## Control theory

Provides tools for stability, feedback, dynamics, and coordination.

## Communication systems

Addresses messaging, latency, reliability, topology, and information sharing.

## Human-robot interaction

Studies how people supervise, trust, understand, and collaborate with robotic systems.

## Safety engineering

Focuses on hazard analysis, failure modes, risk reduction, and assurance.

## Governance and ethics

Examines accountability, privacy, regulation, social impact, and responsible deployment.
