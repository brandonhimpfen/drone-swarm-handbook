# Search-and-Rescue Support

Drone swarms may support search-and-rescue teams by improving coverage, situational awareness, and environmental assessment. This section is conceptual and simulation-focused.

## Potential value

- Faster coverage of large simulated areas
- Distributed sensing models
- Redundant observation paths
- Structured telemetry for review
- Support for human decision-makers

## Human role

Search-and-rescue is a human-led domain. Drone systems should support trained responders, not replace judgment, safety procedures, or command structures.

## Simulation scenario

A beginner simulation could model several agents assigned to different regions of a grid, with telemetry used to estimate coverage completeness.

## Safety and privacy considerations

- Avoid collecting unnecessary identifiable data.
- Document data retention.
- Ensure human review of outputs.
- Treat uncertainty carefully.
- Avoid claims that simulated detection equals real-world reliability.
