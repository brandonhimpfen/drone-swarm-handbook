# Infrastructure Inspection

Infrastructure inspection can involve bridges, buildings, power infrastructure, roads, rail corridors, or industrial sites. This handbook treats the topic conceptually and through simulation scenarios.

## Potential value

- Coverage planning
- Redundant observation
- Structured inspection routes
- Simulation of agent assignment
- Telemetry review

## Constraints

Inspection contexts may involve restricted areas, safety hazards, private property, or critical infrastructure. Real-world activity requires authorization, qualified personnel, and compliance with applicable rules.

## Simulation scenario

A safe educational scenario can model a fictional structure and assign simulated agents to coverage zones while logging task completion and boundary warnings.
